Step 1

Tech closes the job in Jobber

Your field tech marks the job complete in the Jobber app, just like they always do. Nothing changes for the crew. The webhook fires the moment the status flips.

Step 2

Line items mapped to QB accounts

JobberInvoice reads each service line, part, and labor entry from the job. It maps them to your QuickBooks income accounts using the rules you set up once. Tax codes applied automatically.

Step 3

Invoice drafts in QuickBooks

A complete invoice draft appears in QB with the right customer, line items, amounts, and tax. No copy-paste, no retyping. If anything doesn't match, you get an alert instead of a broken invoice.

Step 4

Review once, send to customer

The office manager glances at the draft, confirms it looks right, and hits send. What used to take 15 minutes of data entry per job now takes a 10-second review. Customers get invoiced the same day.
